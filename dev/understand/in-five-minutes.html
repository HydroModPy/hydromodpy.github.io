In five minutes#

One map, five sentences. If you read nothing else, read this page.

  1. HydroModPy computes, for a whole catchment and for each month, the level of the groundwater under the ground, from the relief, the properties of the rock and the rain that reaches the water table.

  2. Where that level reaches the ground, water comes out: these points are the springs of the model.

  3. The streams are what these springs feed downhill. On the map, a stream is blue when it flows all year, orange when it flows part of the year, and grey when it never flows.

  4. This map is a demonstration, not a study of the catchment: it was not compared with observations of dry reaches, so it is labelled indicative. Other results carry the labels calibrated or numerical check, and the label says how far to trust them (reliability label).

  5. Every result depends on assumptions and on data, so read its caption, its label and the limits before you decide anything with it.
