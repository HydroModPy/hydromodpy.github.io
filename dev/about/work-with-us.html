Work with us#

HydroModPy is open research software under the EPL-2.0 licence. You can install it and run it on your own catchment without asking anyone. Write to the team when you want to collaborate, use it for a study, or report a problem.

Who to write to#

  • Alexandre Gauvain, alexandre.gauvain.ag@gmail.com

  • Ronan Abhervé, ronan.abherve@inrae.fr

Two public places are better when the answer can help others:

  • GitHub issues for a bug, a missing feature or a wrong page of this site.

  • The Google group for a question about using HydroModPy.

Ask for a study or a collaboration#

The team works in research. A request for a study on your territory is read by the two contacts above, who say whether and how the team can take it on. To help them answer, say:

  1. The question. What you want to know: where the water table is, which streams keep flowing in a dry year, what a change of climate or of use does.

  2. The place. The catchment, with its outlet or a map.

  3. The data you hold. Heads, discharge, geology, pumping, and the licence of each.

  4. The time frame and what you will do with the answer.

Observed data such as a gauged discharge or a mapped stream network are used to compare the model with the field and to calibrate it. They are not inputs of the model. The Understand pages explain what a result shows and what it does not.

Add your work to this site#

A paper that used HydroModPy can join the list of applications and papers. See how to cite HydroModPy in Cite.
