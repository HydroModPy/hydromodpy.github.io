Applications and papers#

Studies that used HydroModPy, newest first. Each row says what HydroModPy did in the study, as far as the paper states it.

Paper

What HydroModPy did

Abhervé et al. (2025). Projected climate change impacts on groundwater-surface water connectivity in a compartmentalized mountain headwater bedrock aquifer. Water Resources Research. doi:10.1029/2025WR040083

Climate-change projections of groundwater and surface water connectivity in a compartmentalized headwater bedrock aquifer.

Marti et al. (2025). Catchment landforms predict groundwater-dependent wetland sensitivity to recharge changes. Hydrology and Earth System Sciences. doi:10.5194/hess-29-5665-2025

Sensitivity of groundwater-dependent wetlands to changes in recharge, from catchment-scale runs.

Abhervé et al. (2024). Improving calibration of groundwater flow models using headwater streamflow intermittence. Hydrological Processes. doi:10.1002/hyp.15167

Calibration of groundwater flow models against the intermittence of headwater streams, with the stream network metric of HydroModPy.

Floriancic et al. (2024). Evidence of Groundwater Seepage and Mixing at the Vicinity of a Knickpoint in a Mountain Stream. Geophysical Research Letters. doi:10.1029/2024GL111325

Groundwater seepage and mixing at the knickpoint of a mountain stream in Switzerland, run with the Boussinesq solver.

Le Mesnil et al. (2024). Characterizing coastal aquifer heterogeneity from a single piezometer head chronicle. Journal of Hydrology. doi:10.1016/j.jhydrol.2024.131859

Heterogeneity of a coastal aquifer read from the head series of a single piezometer, with HydroModPy as the forward model of the inverse problem.

Abhervé et al. (2023). Calibration of groundwater seepage against the spatial distribution of the stream network to assess catchment-scale hydraulic properties. Hydrology and Earth System Sciences. doi:10.5194/hess-27-3221-2023

The calibration of seepage against the spatial distribution of the stream network, the method behind the network criterion of HydroModPy.

The tutorial of Get started and the pages of Understand follow one catchment, the Nançon (example 04).

Add a study#

Add the paper to docs/source/theory/references.bib, then one row to docs/source/about/applications.yaml with its BibTeX key and one sentence on what HydroModPy did. Open a pull request, or write to the contacts of Work with us. Name a site only when the paper names it.

To cite HydroModPy itself, see Cite. The references behind the equations and the methods are in the bibliography of the theory.
