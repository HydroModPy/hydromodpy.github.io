Notation#

Every symbol of the theory pages, with its unit and the key that sets it. The tables are generated from one registry, docs/source/theory/symbols.yaml, and each theory page renders its own table from the same file.

The units are the ones the code stores: lengths in metres, hydraulic conductivity in m/s, rates in m/s. The soil reservoir is the exception, because it works in mm and days. A name that exists in the code or in an output file is given in code font in the meaning column, so a symbol can be matched with a column of a result file.

Conventions#

  • Lengths are in metres, and elevation is positive up.

  • A flux into the aquifer is positive. The residual of the Boussinesq solver is the one exception: it counts a loss as positive, and its page says so.

  • Cells are \(n\) and \(m\) on the pages of the two MODFLOW solvers and of the mesh, and \(i\) on the Boussinesq page, which runs on triangles.

  • A symbol with an overbar, such as \(\bar B\), is an average over the catchment. It is by area, unless the page says otherwise.

  • A TOML value can carry its unit, such as "500 m" or "1 month". The unit of a key is on its card.

The descent and the distance between networks#

The descent \(D\) is where water goes on the model top: from each cell, the path to a lower neighbour, down to the outlet or to a cell where water leaves the model.
