Theory#

Theory covers what HydroModPy computes or decides: the rules it applies when it builds a model, the conversions from TOML to solver input, the methods it implements itself, and what it refuses. The equations of MODFLOW and of the literature are read at their source. A page gives external theory a few sentences and a citation, then says what HydroModPy does with it. Each page names the keys that set its terms; the steps of a run are in the staircase.

Sources we build on#

  • MODFLOW 6 groundwater flow: Langevin et al. [2017]. Framework and IMS solver: Hughes et al. [2017]. Input guide: MODFLOW 6 input reference.

  • XT3D on irregular meshes: Provost et al. [2017].

  • MODFLOW-NWT: Niswonger et al. [2011], on Harbaugh [2005]. Input guide: MODFLOW-NWT guide.

  • Mesh generation: Gmsh manual.

The solvers of the aquifer gathers the links package by package. Every citation is listed in the Bibliography.
