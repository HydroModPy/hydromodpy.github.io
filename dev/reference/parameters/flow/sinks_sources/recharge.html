Path
flow.sinks_sources.recharge.first_clim
More

'mean' is the mean of the per-period rates, each weighted by its length; 'first' is the rate of period 0; a number is that rate, in the units of values. A transient period keeps its own rate. The same rule holds for one value per period and for one grid per period, under every solver. With negative_to_evt, the steady period takes the rule on the recharge part and on the deficit part, so its net rate is the one stated here. Boussinesq has no EVT and refuses a negative number.

# method = "fao56"Storage law of the soil, the one thing that differs between methods. experimental
Path
flow.sinks_sources.recharge.soil.method
More

"fao56" lowers ETR linearly below alpha x ru (Allen et al. 1998). "thornthwaite" meets the full demand while water is left (Thornthwaite 1948). "dingman" empties the soil as an exponential of the deficit on dry days (Dingman 2008). "edijatno_michel" is the production store of GR4J without its percolation (Edijatno and Michel 1989). The sealed share, the curve number, the bypass, the transit and the snow work the same around every law.

Accepted
string
Example
"fao56", "thornthwaite"

flow.sinks_sources.recharge.soil.alpha
More

Below alpha x ru, ETR falls linearly with the stored water. It is 1 - p of FAO-56 and LP of HBV. The default 0.45 is a choice: it means p = 0.55, the value of FAO-56 table 22 for cereals and maize (pasture 0.60). Above 0, at most 1. Kept fixed by default: on the discharge alone it trades off with ru, since both move the annual volume.

# spinup = "first_year"State of the soil on the first day. experimental
Path
flow.sinks_sources.recharge.soil.spinup
More

"first_year" repeats the first 365 days of the run until the storage at the end of that year moves by less than 0.1 mm, 20 times at most; the run must last 364 days (52 weeks) at least, and a 364-day run repeats those 364 days. "none" starts from a full soil, the state of a soil in a wet winter. Beside a cyclic spin-up of the aquifer (flow.ic.kind = "spinup_cyclic"), the two starts are computed over two windows: the soil settles on the first year, the heads on the whole run. That pair is allowed without a warning, because it is sometimes what the study wants.

Values
  • "first_year" : Repeat the first year until the soil storage settles.
  • "none" : Start from a full soil and an empty transit store.
Accepted
"first_year" | "none"
