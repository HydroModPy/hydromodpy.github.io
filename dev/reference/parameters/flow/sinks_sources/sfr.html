flow.sinks_sources.sfr.<name>.bed_incision
More

A bed never sits above the ground of its own cell, whether this is set or not. None (default) only lifts the lower bound: the bed may then sink to the cell bottom instead of stopping at max_bed_sag. When set, each reach is solved inside [cell_top - bed_incision - max_bed_sag, cell_top - bed_incision], monotone downstream, and the build refuses when that band is empty. rtp is delineated on the model top, so this anchors a bed that a coarse DEM cannot resolve; it is not a correction for the descent D, which never reaches the bed. Pick it against the water table, not against the channel: MODFLOW 6 switches a reach between connected and disconnected at rtp minus streambed_thickness (gwf-sfr.f90:3973-3985), so a bed sitting near the seasonal water-table depth toggles at every outer iteration.

Path
flow.sinks_sources.sfr.<name>.rectify_on_mesh
More

From every delineated cell the descent D, a filled copy of the model top, is traced one neighbour at a time until it reaches a lake, the outlet of D, or an already-traced cell. The union of those paths is the channel: one cell wide (a single downstream per cell, so no braiding), continuous on the descent graph (shared faces, or shared nodes on a quad mesh), following the thalweg of D (so the surface flow follows the reach), and always reaching a real sink (no inland dead-end that leaks its flow out). The streambed top is read on the model top, never on D. D is a copy and never returns to the solver.

Accepted
true | false
