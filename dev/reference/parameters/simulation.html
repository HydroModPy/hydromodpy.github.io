# coverage_policy = "error"What happens to a stress period that a forcing record does not cover.
Path
simulation.time.coverage_policy
More

A sample is the rate of the interval up to the next sample, and the last sample stands for one step of the record (the median spacing of its dates). A period that no sample reaches, before the first date or after the end of the last step, is not covered. 'error' stops the run, 'warn' warns once per record, 'ignore' says nothing; under 'warn' and 'ignore' the period takes the nearest sample in time. The rule is the same for a series, a station and a grid: rain, ETP, recharge, runoff and the declared CSV forcings read as rates (aggregate = 'mean').

Values
  • "error"
  • "warn"
  • "ignore"
Accepted
"error" | "warn" | "ignore"
Example
"error", "warn"
# substeps_per_period = 1Number of solver time steps within each stress period, the one knob that buys transient accuracy. >= 1 dev
Path
simulation.time.substeps_per_period
More

The default of 1 integrates a whole period in a single backward-Euler step, which is not a neutral choice: on a seasonally recharged hillslope drained by DRN cells it moves the monthly discharge by 4 to 28 % against a refined run, across the whole plausible bedrock range, and a calibration scored on discharge absorbs that into the fitted parameters. The right count compares the aquifer response time tau = L^2 S / (K b) with the period length, and the solver cost is linear in it; 30 gives daily steps inside monthly periods. Consumed by the MODFLOW backends as DIS NSTP / TDIS nstp, and by the Boussinesq backend, which solves each period in as many backward-Euler steps of equal length. Every solver stores one result per period: the head at the end of the period and each flux as the mean of its steps, weighted by their length.

Accepted
integer
Valid range
>= 1 (refused outside)

Mesh geometry, topography, layer thickness, indices, coordinates and timestamps are never rounded. simulation.results.persistence.save_parquet save_parquet true Persist per-simulation tabular outputs (timeseries, budgets, mass_balance) as Parquet files. simulation.results.persistence.compression compression “zstd” none zstd lz4 gzip snappy Codec DECLARED for Zarr field arrays and Parquet tables. The writers carry their own codec (zstd) and do not read this field, so changing it changes nothing today; it records the intent and is the field a writer would read once the choice is threaded through. simulation.results.persistence.compression_level compression_level 5 Compression level DECLARED for those writers. Same as the codec: core/io/parquet.py and core/io/geoparquet.py hold level 5 and do not read this field. The default says 5 rather than 3 so the declaration at least matches the bytes actually written.
